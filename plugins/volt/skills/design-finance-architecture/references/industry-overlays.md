# Nonbank extension pattern

Reuse the canonical event/policy/measurement/journal/reporting chain, then license and validate product-specific
overlays. Do not reuse mortgage policies for another industry.

| Industry | Example business events | Accounting / EPM design focus |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS / technology | Contract signed, performance delivered, usage measured, credit/refund, contract modification | Contract liabilities/assets, service/product revenue, capitalized contract/development costs where eligible; ARR is a management measure, not automatically GAAP revenue |
| Manufacturing | Purchase receipt, production issue/completion, scrap, sale, inventory count | Inventory/COGS, WIP, variances and cost method; unit costing and inventory measurement owned by approved services; production/margin planning |
| Retail | Sale, return, loyalty issuance/redemption, marketplace settlement | Gross/net principal-agent decisions, tax, inventory and loyalty obligations; channel profitability and demand planning |
| Healthcare | Treatment delivered, claim submitted/adjusted, remittance | Payer/reimbursement and contractual-adjustment policies; jurisdiction-specific privacy; capacity and service-line planning |
| Professional services | Time approved, milestone accepted, billing, collection | Contract assets/unbilled revenue, revenue recognition and labor allocations; utilization and workforce planning |

For each overlay deliver a source-backed policy matrix, lifecycle catalog, golden cases, regulator/tax applicability,
platform mapping, reporting drivers, owner and release gates. Accounting-standard convergence does not establish
identical application. Obtain explicit jurisdiction and product scope rather than treating industry label as a
complete policy.
