## 1. Effective dates and transition status

| Standard | Mandated effective (annual periods beginning on/after) | Early adoption | Transitional notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| IFRS 9 Financial Instruments | 1 January 2018 | — | Substantially completed globally; amendments continue (e.g., 2022 amendments on classification of financial assets with ESG-linked features; 2023 amendments on lease liabilities in supplier finance and derecognition — vendor finance disclosure) |
| IFRS 16 Leases | 1 January 2019 | — | Fully effective; later amendments: COVID-19 rent concessions (2020–2021), narrow amendments on sale and leaseback (2022), supplier finance arrangements (2023–2024) |
| IFRS 17 Insurance Contracts | 1 January 2023 | Permitted (including with IFRS 9 pre-2018) | Replaces IFRS 4; transition approaches FRA / MRA / FVA in Appendix C; IFRS 9 comparative overlay permitted for financial assets on initial adoption |
| IFRS 18 Presentation & Disclosure | 1 January 2027 | Permitted | Replaces IAS 1; restate prior-period comparatives; MPM note required; related amendments to IAS 7, IFRS 7, IAS 34, IAS 8 |

**Practical flag**: as of November 2026, IFRS 18 is adopted by early adopters only — for periods before 2027, IAS 1 still governs presentation but IFRS 18 planning (P&L mapping, MPM inventory, subtotal design) should already be underway.

Also relevant: IFRS 7 (disclosures for IFRS 9 instruments), IFRS 13 (fair value), IAS 32 (offsetting, classification), IAS 21 (FX), IAS 8 (accounting policies, changes in estimates, errors), IAS 10 (events after reporting), IAS 33 (EPS), IAS 36 (impairment of non-financial assets — ROU assets), IAS 37 (restoration provisions in ROU), IFRS 13/IAS 28 (associates).

## 2. Standard-to-standard interaction matrix

| Interaction | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| IFRS 9 vs IFRS 17 scope | Insurance contract (significant insurance risk) → IFRS 17; investment contract without insurance risk → IFRS 9; investment contract with DPF → IFRS 17 |
| IFRS 9 vs IFRS 16 | Lease receivables → IFRS 9 ECL; lease liabilities → IFRS 16; net investment in finance lease → IFRS 9 impairment |
| Insurer holds financial assets | IFRS 9 governs measurement; IFRS 17 presentation governs where the income appears (investing for assets backing insurance liabilities) |
| IFRS 16 vs IFRS 18 | Lessee: ROU depreciation operating; liability interest and principal financing; variable payments operating |
| IFRS 17 vs IFRS 18 | Insurance service result operating; insurance finance investing; reinsurance net presentation separate |
| Supplier finance arrangements | IFRS 7 44E–44H disclosure; IFRS 9 derecognition test for payables sold into programs; IFRS 16 lease liability amendment (2024) |
| IFRS 15 vs IFRS 17 | Fixed-fee service contracts with no significant insurance risk → IFRS 15 |
| Business combinations | IFRS 3: contingent consideration FVTPL; acquired insurance contracts remeasured under IFRS 17 transition-like rules (FVA) |
| Tax | IAS 12 applies to temporary differences from IFRS 9/16/17 measurements (e.g., ROU/lease liability, loss allowances, CSM) |

## 3. Common pitfalls and judgment hotspots

1. **IFRS 9 SPPI on ESG/climate-linked terms** — 2022 amendments clarify that contractual terms changing cash flows based on ESG targets may still meet SPPI if cash flows do not leverage climate risk; assess case by case.
2. **SICR assessments that never migrate to Stage 2** — audit focus: evidence-based triggers, not just 30-day past due; incorporate forward-looking info.
3. **Lifetime ECL for "open" exposures** — loan commitments and revolving facilities: estimate beyond contractual notice period where the entity cannot demand repayment; behavioural life.
4. **Lease term optimism** — renewal options included without "reasonably certain" support; IBR estimation unsupported (credit spread + term-specific adjustments).
5. **Separating lease components vs expedient** — lessee expedient elected without disclosure; sale-and-leaseback gains recognised fully instead of only on rights transferred.
6. **IFRS 17 annual cohorts** — grouping contracts across issue years; coverage units changing without pattern update; VFA applied to contracts without substantial participation.
7. **OCI option inconsistency** — switching between P&L and OCI for rate effects between portfolios or periods.
8. **MPMs under IFRS 18** — KPIs used in investor decks that are subtotals but not disclosed in the single MPM note; reconciliation missing tax/NCI effects.
9. **Effective dates** — do not apply IFRS 18 to 2026 statements; do not apply IFRS 17 mechanics to non-insurers.
10. **Materiality** — checklist-driven boilerplate disclosures; IFRS 18 requires entity-specific, materiality-based notes.

## 4. Key terms glossary

- **CSM (contractual service margin)** — unearned profit component of the liability for remaining coverage under IFRS 17; recognised as services are provided.
- **Coverage units** — quantity of benefits provided under a group of insurance contracts, driving CSM release.
- **DPF (discretionary participation feature)** — contractual right to receive additional benefits whose amount/timing is at the issuer's discretion.
- **ECL (expected credit loss)** — probability-weighted estimate of credit losses discounted at the original EIR.
- **FRA / MRA / FVA** — IFRS 17 transition approaches: full retrospective / modified retrospective / fair value approach.
- **GMM / VFA / PAA** — IFRS 17 measurement models: general / variable fee / premium allocation approach.
- **IBR (incremental borrowing rate)** — lessee-specific discount rate used when the rate implicit in the lease is not readily determinable.
- **MPM (management-defined performance measure)** — IFRS 18 subtotal used in public communications, disclosed in a single note with reconciliation.
- **POCI** — purchased or originated credit-impaired financial asset.
- **ROU asset** — lessee's right-of-use asset under IFRS 16.
- **SICR** — significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition (IFRS 9 ECL staging trigger).
- **SPPI** — solely payments of principal and interest (IFRS 9 classification test).
