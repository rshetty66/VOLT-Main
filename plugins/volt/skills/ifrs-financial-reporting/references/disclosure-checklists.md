# Disclosure checklists — IFRS 9 / 16 / 17 / 18

Baseline for full-IFRS listed entities. Apply materiality (IAS 1 / IFRS 18); remove inapplicable items rather than boilerplating. Each entry maps to the primary standard; use with the technical references for paragraph detail.

## IFRS 9 / IFRS 7 (financial instruments)

**Significance and accounting policies**
- [ ] Classes of financial instruments by category (AC / FVOCI / FVTPL; FL AC / FVTPL)
- [ ] Accounting policy summary: initial measurement, subsequent measurement, ECL methodology, hedge accounting policy
- [ ] Financial assets/liabilities at FVTPL (including designated under FV option; own credit OCI policy for designated liabilities)

**Credit risk / ECL (IFRS 7 35A–35T, 35M–35N)**
- [ ] Loss allowance reconciliation by class and stage (1/2/3)
- [ ] Explanation of inputs, assumptions and estimation techniques (PD/LGD/EAD, forward-looking scenarios)
- [ ] SICR assessment approach; staging criteria and quantitative thresholds (30 days past due indicator)
- [ ] Credit quality analysis by internal grade/external rating; collateral and mitigation
- [ ] POCI assets: gross carrying amount and accumulated loss allowance
- [ ] Write-offs and recoveries; amounts still subject to enforcement

**Liquidity / market risk (IFRS 7 34, 39–42)**
- [ ] Maturity analysis of financial liabilities (undiscounted; derivative liabilities; loan commitments)
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis for market risks (currency, interest rate, other price)

**Fair value and offsetting (IFRS 7 25–30, 42A–42H)**
- [ ] Fair value hierarchy by level (1/2/3); transfers between levels and policy
- [ ] Level 3 roll-forward; significant unobservable inputs and sensitivity
- [ ] Carrying amount vs fair value for AC items (when not approximated)
- [ ] Offsetting disclosures: gross/determinable amounts, master netting arrangements

**Hedge accounting (IFRS 7 21A–24F)**
- [ ] Risk management strategy and objectives for each type of hedge
- [ ] Notional and maturity profile of hedging instruments; average rates/prices
- [ ] Hedging instruments and hedged items by balance-sheet line; hedge ineffectiveness recognised in P&L
- [ ] Effect of hedging on financial position and performance

**Modifications / derecognition**
- [ ] Modification gains/losses not resulting in derecognition
- [ ] Derecognition: transferred assets, continuing involvement (carrying amounts, max exposure, collateral), asset-backed securitisation structures

## IFRS 16 (leases)

**Lessee**
- [ ] Maturity analysis of lease liabilities (undiscounted; by period band)
- [ ] Reconciliation of opening/closing ROU assets by class of asset
- [ ] Expense: short-term leases; low-value asset leases; variable lease payments not included in the liability
- [ ] Income from subleasing ROU assets; total cash outflow for leases
- [ ] Gains/losses on sale and leaseback transactions
- [ ] Maturity analysis of commitments for short-term/low-value leases (if material)

**Lessor**
- [ ] Maturity analysis of lease receivables (undiscounted) and reconciliation of undiscounted to net investment
- [ ] Unearned finance income on the net investment in finance leases
- [ ] Operating lease income; qualitative/quantitative risk exposure
- [ ] Manufacturer/dealer lessor: selling profit/loss, finance income, market-rate adjustments

## IFRS 17 (insurance contracts)

**Reconciliations and roll-forwards**
- [ ] Reconciliation of LRC and LIC (claims) between opening and closing, split by measurement model (GMM/VFA/PAA)
- [ ] CSM roll-forward: new business, interest accretion, changes relating to current/future service, CSM release, FX, business combinations
- [ ] Transition: transition CSM and loss component by transition approach (FRA/MRA/FVA); impact on equity at transition
- [ ] Onerous contracts: loss component roll-forward, amounts recognised/reversed in P&L

**Judgments and methods**
- [ ] Methods to estimate FCF: cash flow estimation process, discount-rate determination (top-down/bottom-up), risk-adjustment technique
- [ ] Assessment that contracts meet PAA eligibility; VFA eligibility
- [ ] Grouping approach and annual cohort policy; contract boundary assumptions
- [ ] Coverage-unit methodology (quantity of benefits, pattern)
- [ ] OCI option election and effect of discount-rate changes on P&L vs OCI

**Other**
- [ ] Insurance revenue disaggregation (if material by product/region); investment components excluded from revenue
- [ ] Effect of insurance contracts on the statement of financial position by model
- [ ] Reinsurance held: assets by type, reinsurance finance income/expense, loss-recovery component
- [ ] Significant judgements in applying the standard (aggregation, discount rates, VFA mirroring, risk mitigation option)
- [ ] Income and expenses by insurance line vs assets backing liabilities (for IFRS 18 operating/investing alignment)

## IFRS 18 (presentation and disclosure, effective 2027)

**Statement of profit or loss**
- [ ] Required subtotals: operating profit/loss; profit/loss before financing and income tax; profit/loss
- [ ] Classification of every income/expense line into operating/investing/financing; consistency with cash-flow categories
- [ ] Specified main business activity disclosures (banks/insurers/customer financiers): how classification was applied
- [ ] Additional subtotals/aggregations used, labelled and reconciled

**MPM note (single note)**
- [ ] Each MPM: label, description, why useful
- [ ] Reconciliation to nearest IFRS subtotal: each reconciling item, income tax effect, NCI effect
- [ ] Statement that MPMs are not IFRS measures; changes to MPMs

**Notes and disaggregation**
- [ ] Disaggregation in notes of: D&A; employee benefits; inventory write-downs; impairment of non-financial assets; FX gains/losses; FVTPL gains/losses (incl. derivatives); IFRS 9 impairment/derecognition/modification gains-losses; hedge instrument gains/losses; gains/losses on fair value less costs of disposal
- [ ] Expense nature/function analysis and the related note disaggregation
- [ ] Material accounting policy information (IAS 8, revised by IFRS 18)
- [ ] Comparative information restated; transition disclosure: reconciliation of IAS 1 P&L to IFRS 18 categories
